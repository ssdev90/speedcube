Welcome to the Game

Welcome to your personal training manual. You are about to learn a system built completely on rhythm and muscle memory. No crazy math, just beats.

The Secret Cheat Code:

Mistakes are 100% allowed. If your hands slip, you never have to start from zero. You just hit the "Panic Button" (Next Step), figure out which checkpoint you are at, and keep going. You've got this!

The Laws of the Cube

1

Centers NEVER Move

The middle piece on every side has only one color. The White center is ALWAYS opposite Yellow. If the center is Blue, you are building the "Blue Room."

2

Edges vs. Corners

Edges have 2 colors and live in the middle of a row. Corners have 3 colors and live on the pointy corners.

3

The "Anchor"

Once you finish Step 2, the White Center piece will point at the floor forever. Yellow points at the ceiling. Never flip the cube over unless the guide tells you to!

The Panic Button

If you lose your place, get confused, or turn the wrong side: STOP.
Do not randomly twist the cube trying to fix it. Take a deep breath. It is completely okay!

How to save your game:

  1. Look at your cube.
  2. Read the Reverse Checks in this guide, starting from Step 8 and reading backward (Step 8, Step 7, Step 6...).
  3. Find the highest step where your cube still matches the picture and description.
  4. Do a Bootcamp Drill in the air to reset your hands, and start again from that safe checkpoint.

Warm Up Phase

Drill 1: The Righty Trigger

Rule: Do not try to solve the cube until you pass this drill. Think of this like stretching before a sport.

Hold the cube. Your left hand stays frozen.

BEAT 1 Push Away

Right hand pushes the right side AWAY from you (up).

BEAT 2 Flick Left

Right index finger flicks the top side to the LEFT.

BEAT 3 Pull Towards

Right hand pulls the right side TOWARDS you (down).

BEAT 4 Flick Right

Left index finger flicks the top side back to the RIGHT.

The Test

Do this Push, Flick, Pull, Flick rhythm 6 times in a row on a solved (or partially solved) cube. If you do it right, the pieces will return to exactly how they started. It proves your hands have the memory!

Warm Up Phase

Drill 2: The Lefty Trigger

Rule: This is the exact mirror. Your right hand stays completely frozen.

BEAT 1 Push Away

Left hand pushes the left side AWAY from you (up).

BEAT 2 Flick Right

Left index finger flicks the top side to the RIGHT.

BEAT 3 Pull Towards

Left hand pulls the left side TOWARDS you (down).

BEAT 4 Flick Left

Right index finger flicks the top side back to the LEFT.

Awesome job!

Do this 6 times in a row until it feels natural. Your hands are officially ready to start the game.

Level 1

Step 1: The Daisy

Your Goal

Make a flower on the top face. Yellow center in the middle, surrounded by 4 White edges. (Ignore the corners completely).

Action

This is the only step where you can just mess around and experiment! Find white edges anywhere on the cube and twist them up so they surround the yellow center.

Checkpoint

Look at the top. It looks exactly like a Daisy.

Reverse Check

This is the start. If it's messed up, just build the Daisy again!

Top View

Level 2

Step 2: The White Cross

Your Goal

Move the white petals to the bottom to make a White Cross.

Action
  1. Look at the side color of one of your white petals.
  2. Twist the top two layers until that side color matches its Center piece.
  3. Once matched, turn that entire face around twice (180 degrees) so the white piece drops all the way down to the bottom.
  4. Repeat for all 4 petals.
Checkpoint

Look at the bottom face. It is a White Cross. Look at the sides: the bottom two pieces of every side match colors perfectly.

Reverse Check

If the top is no longer a Daisy and the bottom isn't a cross, you are back at Step 1.

🔥 Great start! From now on, the White Cross faces the floor. Yellow faces the ceiling. 🔥

Bottom View

Level 3

Step 3: First Layer Corners

Your Goal

Solve the entire bottom layer. You are doing amazing!

Action
  • Find a corner on the top layer that has White on it. Look at its other two colors (like Blue and Red).
  • Turn the top until that corner is sitting directly between the Blue and Red centers on the sides.
  • Hold the cube so that corner is in the front-right position.

    Do your Righty Trigger (Drill 1) over and over until that white corner piece locks into the bottom, pointing at the floor.
  • Repeat for all 4 corners.
Checkpoint

The entire bottom face is solid White.

Look at the sides: the bottom row forms a little upside-down "T" shape that matches the center color.

Reverse Check

If the white bottom is broken or missing pieces, go back to Step 2.

Side View
(Upside-down T)

Level 4

Step 4: Second Layer Edges

Your Goal

Solve the middle row. You are almost halfway done!

Action

Find an edge piece on the top that does NOT have Yellow. Look at its front color, match it to its center (making a tall upside-down T). Look at its top color. It needs to fall to the left or right side.

The Push Away Rule:

Always push the top layer AWAY from the side it needs to go to.

If it goes Right:

Push top Left. Do Righty Trigger. Turn the whole cube to look at the right side. Do Lefty Trigger.

If it goes Left:

Push top Right. Do Lefty Trigger. Turn the whole cube to look at the left side. Do Righty Trigger.

Checkpoint

The bottom TWO rows of the entire cube are completely solved. Look at that progress!

Reverse Check

If the solid white bottom is broken, go back to Step 3.

Side View
(Two Layers Done)

Level 5

Step 5: The Yellow Cross

Your Goal

Make a Yellow Cross on top.

Action

Look at the yellow top. You will see a dot, an "L" shape, or a horizontal line. Ignore the corners.

Repeat this combo until you have a cross:

  1. Turn the FRONT face of the cube clockwise (like turning a car steering wheel to the right).
  2. Do the Righty Trigger.
  3. Turn the FRONT face counter-clockwise (steering wheel left) to fix the white bottom.
Checkpoint

Yellow Cross on top. Bottom two layers are still perfectly safe.

Reverse Check

If the bottom two layers are scrambled, you forgot to turn the steering wheel back left. Drop back to Step 4.

Top View
(Yellow Cross)

Level 6

Step 6: Match Yellow Edges

Your Goal

The sides of the yellow cross must match their centers.

Action

Twist the top until exactly TWO edges match their centers. (If all 4 match, skip this step!). Hold the cube so one matching edge is at the BACK, and one is on the RIGHT.

The "Sune" Rhythm:

Right Push Away
Top Flick Left
Right Pull Towards
Top Flick Left
Right Push Away
Top DOUBLE Flick Left
Right Pull Towards
Checkpoint

Yellow cross on top, and all four side edges match the centers perfectly.

Reverse Check

If the yellow cross broke, drop back to Step 5.

Level 7

Step 7: Position Yellow Corners

Your Goal

Put the final 4 corners in the correct neighborhoods.

They DO NOT need to have yellow facing up yet, they just need to have the right three colors for the corner they are sitting in.

Action

Find ONE corner that is in the correct spot. Hold it in the Front-Right.

The Swapper Rhythm:

Top Flick Left
Right Push Away
Top Flick Right
Left Push Away
Top Flick Left
Right Pull Towards
Top Flick Right
Left Pull Towards

Check if the corners are in the right spots. If not, do it one more time.

Checkpoint

Every corner has the correct three colors for the space it is sitting in. You are ONE step away!

Reverse Check

If the cross edges don't match their centers anymore, drop back to Step 6.

⚠️ FINAL LEVEL ⚠️

Step 8: The Boss Fight

READ THIS FIRST

The cube is going to look completely scrambled and broken during this step. DO NOT PANIC. Your brain will tell you something is wrong, but your hands know what to do. Trust your muscle memory. You are so close!

Your Goal

Make the yellow stickers face up to win.

Action
  1. Turn the entire cube UPSIDE DOWN. White is now on the ceiling. Yellow is on the floor.
  2. Look at the front-right corner on the bottom. Is it yellow? If not, do the Righty Trigger over and over until the yellow sticker faces the floor.
  3. The Golden Rule: Do NOT turn the whole cube to get to the next corner! ONLY spin the bottom layer (the floor) with your finger to bring the next unsolved corner into the front-right spot.
  4. Repeat Righty Triggers until yellow points down. Repeat for all corners.

YOU DID IT!

The cube is solved.

Reverse Check

If the cube is totally scrambled after this, you probably accidentally turned the whole cube instead of just the bottom layer. Take a deep breath. You proved you can get to the Boss Fight, which is amazing. Scramble it up, do your Bootcamp Drills, and crush it on your next run.
